Key Data Flows
Client Posts a Job
1
Client fills /client/new-request form
2
createJobWithTriage server action fires
3
Job inserted to Supabase as pending_triage
4
Claude Sonnet diagnoses, returns DIY/pro + cost
5
Job updated to triaged, costs saved in cents
Tradesman Claims a Job
1
Tradesman browses /tradesman/jobs
2
Clicks Claim → ClaimButton fires claimJob()
3
Server Action updates jobs.status → matched
4
tradesman_id written, page revalidated
Guest AI Diagnosis
1
Guest visits /diagnose (no account needed)
2
Describes issue, selects urgency
3
runGuestTriage() calls Claude directly
4
Returns DIY steps or professional recommendation
5
CTA prompts guest to sign up to book a plumber
